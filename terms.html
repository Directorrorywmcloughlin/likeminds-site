The small print

Pre-order terms.

Pre-orders, delivery and returns for Like Minds merch. Last updated November 2026.

Who you're buying from

Like Minds Global FZ-LLC, licence no. 45036142, Compass Building, Al Hulaila Industrial Zone FZ, Ras Al Khaimah, UAE. Questions about an order: hello@likeminds.ae.

How the pre-order works

  • You pay in full when you pre-order. Prices are in UAE dirhams (AED).
  • Nothing is made until the pre-order closes. We then book production for the items and sizes that were ordered.
  • Payments are taken by Stripe. We never see or store your card details.
  • You'll get a receipt by email straight away, and a second email with your delivery date once production is booked.

Delivery and collection

  • Made-to-order items take time. We'll confirm a delivery date by email after the pre-order closes. If that date moves, we'll tell you why and give you the new date.
  • Collect free at a Like Minds night, or choose delivery within the UAE at checkout.
  • If production can't go ahead, for any reason, you get a full refund.

Changing or cancelling

  • Until the pre-order closes, you can change your size or fit, or cancel for a full refund. Email us with your receipt.
  • Once production is booked, your item is being made for you, so we can't cancel the order.

Returns

  • Faulty or wrong item: tell us within 14 days of receiving it, with a photo. We'll replace it or refund you in full.
  • Wrong size: tell us within 14 days. If we have your size spare, we'll swap it. If we don't, we'll do our best but can't promise a swap, as items are made to order.
  • Items must be unworn, unwashed and with tags on, except for faults.
  • Refunds go back to the card you paid with. Your bank may take a few working days to show it.

Sizing and fit

Tees come in two fits, clearly labelled: Fitted and Oversized. A size guide goes up with the artwork. If you're between sizes, ask us before you order.

Your details

We use your name, email and delivery details only to make and deliver your order and to contact you about it. See the privacy note on likeminds.ae.

The law

These terms are governed by the laws of the United Arab Emirates. Nothing here limits your rights under UAE consumer protection law.
