Founder's note

Four years
in.

Like Minds has been running for four years now.

I wanted to create a series of designs that recognise my own personal punk rock and hardcore roots and how that's transcribed into the nature of the Like Minds community. The artwork in this merch series is inspired by that subculture while also creating a sense of place through the flora and fauna featured.

For many, including myself, Like Minds has created a sense of belonging.

Rory McLoughlin · Founder, Like Minds
